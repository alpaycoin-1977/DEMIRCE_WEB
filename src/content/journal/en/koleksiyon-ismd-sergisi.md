DEMİRCE was the 10th guest of **“Koleksiyon / İzmir SMD Hosts Architects”**, an exhibition series by the İzmir Association of Independent Architects and Koleksiyon. The show, featuring **15 projects**, ran at Koleksiyon’s Mithatpaşa space until 20 November.

Described in the press as “known for projects that make a difference in İzmir and winner of the 2007 CNBC Best Architecture award”, the studio was represented by Alpay Demirci:

> DEMİRCE is not just the name of an office; for us it is a way of life. We have a few core principles: functionality, innovation, simplicity, integrating with the way people live, and adding value to the surroundings.
