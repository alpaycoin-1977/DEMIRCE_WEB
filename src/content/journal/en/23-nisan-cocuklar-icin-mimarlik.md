To celebrate 23 April, National Sovereignty and Children’s Day, DEMİRCE Norms designed a special programme for children from Montekid Sahilevleri Montessori Kindergarten and Ekin College’s Seyrek and Ataşehir campuses.

Under the motto **“The future is created today”**, the day opened with an architectural fable. Çakır Dilek Yunar of Lavisio Corporate Development Centre told a story designed to encourage divergent thinking.

The houses the children drew from their own imaginations were then modelled digitally with DEMİRCE architects and, with the support of Can Dijital, **3D-printed**. The houses were finally rearranged into a small town of their own.

The children shared their experience in a short interview and left with their prints, images of their town and certificates. We celebrate the day of all the world’s children with love.
