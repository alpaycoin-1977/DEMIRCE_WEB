People living modern lives increasingly turn to spa and wellness centres to escape stress and find calm. That is why aesthetics and function must be considered together in spa design.

Interior planning — and an architecture that allows for it — along with every detail should be shaped around helping guests find peace. Natural materials, pastel tones and ergonomic furniture play a key role in creating a spacious, serene atmosphere.

The overall architecture should also make the best use of daylight to improve the energy efficiency of the interior. Designed this way, these spaces raise quality of life and support both physical and mental wellbeing.
