Located in Torbalı, İzmir, this factory belongs to a leading manufacturer of fire-extinguishing products and systems. With prestige in mind, the façades of the administrative wing were designed as a **double skin**.

On this project in Ayrancılar, the façade design, planning and interior solutions are ours. We moved forward step by step — out of love for design, the wish to put something of ourselves into every project, and the impatience of waiting to see the result.
