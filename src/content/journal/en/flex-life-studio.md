We attended the groundbreaking ceremony of Flex Life Studio, a project we have been working on for some time.

Rising in Ulukent, Çiğli, and developed by BYY İnşaat, the project comprises **157 homes** on a **3,500 m²** site — studios, one-bedroom flats and one-bedroom duplexes — with a café, market, covered parking, play areas, sauna, gym and swimming pool.

Our thanks to BYY İnşaat, Oksijen360 for the visualisations, the US Brainworks and AKC Tanıtım teams, Keller Williams İzmir, Çiğli Municipality for a swift permit process, and everyone who joined us at the ceremony.
