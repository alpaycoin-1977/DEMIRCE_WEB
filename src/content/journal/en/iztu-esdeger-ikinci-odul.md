Our entry for the İzmir Tınaztepe University Campus Design Competition, prepared as **DEMİRCE Norms at Architecture & Ofis Vesaire**, was awarded a **joint second prize**.

We proposed a campus that merges with the characteristic topography of the Buca site, revives a dry stream bed and is organised around pedestrian promenades that stay alive at every hour of the day.
