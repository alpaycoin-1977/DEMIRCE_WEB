After a long break, we returned to competitions with the İzmir Development Agency competition. In the same period, our entry for the Ministry of National Education’s **Mersin Mezitli Education Campus** competition was awarded **first prize**.

Many thanks to everyone who organised the competition, and to all the people and institutions who supported the DEMİRCE team while we worked through two competitions at once.

**Project team:** Alpay Demirci, Burçin Demirci, Anıl Binay, Belgin Utma, Gözde Akman, Ecem Ceylan, Burak Bakö, Görkem Kayar, Müjde Yar, Kerem Dumlu
