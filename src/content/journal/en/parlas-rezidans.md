With **Parlas Residence** in Bornova, İzmir, we designed for city dwellers — offering a modern, user-focused way of living to the social, discerning people of İzmir.

Close to the city centre, transport, schools, hospitals, entertainment and shopping, the area is a strong magnet for urban life. We interpreted the project as the young, dynamic and modern lifestyle of the city taking on a **sculptural form**.

Reflecting the diversity of the city’s population, the scheme combines one-bedroom flats with double-height duplexes. Behind a generously glazed façade whose lines echo the movement of city life are an indoor pool, zen pools, gym and lounge.

— Alpay Demirci, architect
